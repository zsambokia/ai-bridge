# Epic — Orki Cognitive Operating System

**Status:** The technical Cognitive Operating System Epic, ORKI-001 through
ORKI-010, is officially accepted by the Product Owner. Its architectural
foundation is complete. The historical DCMI is 66/100. Full live Digital COO
behavioural certification is explicitly not claimed: it remains separate CVO
validation work and cannot be inferred from the accepted technical
architecture.

## Outcome

Rebuild Orki from a conversation/planning wrapper into AI Bridge's Digital COO: evidence-backed Cognitive State, initiative, decision support, evolutionary memory, genuine planning, and provider-independent reasoning.

## Invariants

- Conversation is not state and transcript is not memory.
- LLMs are replaceable reasoning providers; AI Bridge owns behaviour.
- Facts, assumptions, alternatives, trade-offs, recommendations, confidence, and required decisions remain explainable.
- Existing AKB publication, approval, orchestration, contract, execution, evidence, and Release Gate boundaries remain authoritative.

refactor, review, documentation/AKB, independent audit, operational
validation, self-critique, evidence, and Release Gates. Under the active
Product Owner Factory Development Mode authority, ORKI-003 through ORKI-006 do
not require individual Product Owner approval: a full Release Gate PASS starts
the next authorised Sprint automatically.

## Strategic sequence adjustment: ORKI-008 and ORKI-009

Before further Recommendation Intelligence expansion, ORKI-008 introduces the
[Product Owner Cognitive Model](../architecture/ORKI_PRODUCT_OWNER_COGNITIVE_MODEL.md).
It is a separate, evidence-linked model of operational collaboration patterns,
not a transcript, personalisation store, or authority source. The canonical
Cognitive State therefore composes four distinct models: Mission, Project,
Product Owner, and Factory. ORKI-008 independently proved review, correction,
versioning, project-aware isolation, and explainable bounded-context delivery
for a reversible adaptive default.

ORKI-009 is a deliberately bounded evolution of that foundation: it provides
explainable confidence weighting, transparent handling of unscored evidence,
append-only profile history and evidence-backed cognitive drift. It does not
create recommendation, decision, planning, governance or execution authority.

The next reasoning expansion is named the **Operational Reasoning Engine**,
not a standalone recommendation generator. ORKI-010 is the separately scoped,
release-gated Sprint that proves useful, explainable adaptive COO behaviour:
a recommendation is a late result of a validated mission/evidence/unknowns,
three-alternative, trade-off and counter-argument cycle. It cannot affect any
governed path.

## Autonomous execution and Executive Checkpoints

The mandatory [Executive Checkpoint policy](../architecture/ORKI_EXECUTIVE_CHECKPOINTS.md)
creates informational—not approval—reports after ORKI-003 and ORKI-006.
Checkpoint A follows Recommendation Engine and Checkpoint B follows Memory
Intelligence. Neither pauses work. After Checkpoint B, the remaining approved
capability sequence continues automatically when every Sprint Release Gate is
PASS and no genuine business decision, legal/compliance constraint, or
fundamental Epic-scope change is present.

This policy does not weaken any Sprint boundary or Release Gate. It replaces
only the unnecessary per-Sprint Product Owner approval wait with durable
executive visibility.

## Required executable scenarios

Certification covers a new application, existing application, ERP integration, enterprise system, repository takeover, architecture redesign, production incident, Sprint planning, business planning, roadmap creation, risk analysis, technology recommendation, long multi-session memory, knowledge reuse, delivery planning, and operational recovery. Each records input, prior state, evidence, recommendation/decision trace, question count, expected and actual outcome, and reproducible PASS/FAIL evidence.

## Completion gate

Independent evidence must prove consistent Digital COO behaviour: proactive mission understanding, evidence-driven recommendations, genuine plans, reusable correctable knowledge, explainable reasoning, visible operational thinking, intact governance, and no questionnaire, wizard, or prompt-only fake intelligence behaviour.

## Mandatory COO Capability Acceptance

Every Sprint must pass the [COO Capability Acceptance](../architecture/ORKI_COO_CAPABILITY_ACCEPTANCE.md)
gate in addition to all existing engineering, operational, governance, evidence,
documentation, AKB, roadmap, regression, self-critique, and independent-audit
gates. A capability FAIL blocks Sprint completion even when all automated tests
pass. The earlier 85/100 DCMI wording is retained as a long-term
behavioural-maturity target. The 2026-08-02 Product Owner closure directive
separates it from this accepted technical Epic closure: DCMI may increase only
through independently proved behavioural evidence in a separately authorised
validation cycle.
