# Orki Product Owner Scenarios

**Status:** Canonical acceptance scenario catalogue.

Each scenario is executable and records starting Cognitive State, available evidence, transcript or event inputs, recommendations, questions, decision trace, resulting state, DCMI measurements, and PASS/FAIL outcome.

1. Brand new application: turn an uncertain idea into an evidence-aware Mission and recommended first safe action.
2. Existing repository takeover: distinguish repository facts from unknowns, risks, and inherited assumptions.
3. Large ERP extension: assess integration dependencies, alternatives, and operational migration/recovery risk.
4. Business process redesign: challenge a weak premise and propose measurable alternatives.
5. Architecture refactoring: make a reasoned architecture recommendation with rejected options and delivery impact.
6. Production incident: prioritise safe recovery, evidence collection, communication, and follow-up learning.
7. Long-running project evolution: revise Mission and roadmap only through attributable evidence and accepted decisions.
8. Evidence-based recommendation: from the same attributed Mission State,
   explain a prioritised safe recommendation, its facts/inferences,
   assumptions, alternatives, trade-offs, confidence, dependency, and next
   safe action—without creating a decision, plan, or execution.
9. Product Owner operational working relationship: after ten attributable
   interactions, project only evidence-linked operational preferences, show
   their scope and confidence, accept a Product Owner correction, supersede
   the affected revision, and explain a later safe adaptive default. The
   scenario must prove that no raw transcript, personal data, unsupported
   inference, or cross-project leakage is required; the profile cannot create
   a decision, plan, governance approval, or execution.

The scenario suite measures initiative, unnecessary-question count, usefulness of recommendations, Product Owner guidance, explainability, governance discipline, and the DCMI dimensions. It is the authoritative behavioural test surface; provider prose or unit tests alone cannot pass this gate.
