# COO Capability Acceptance

**Status:** Mandatory Release Gate for the Orki Cognitive Operating System Epic.

Engineering PASS alone is insufficient. Every Orki Sprint must produce reproducible evidence against the applicable criteria below. A failed, unmeasured, or inapplicable-without-justification criterion is a Sprint FAIL.

| Capability | Objective acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Mission Understanding | Correctly separates stated intent, inferred constraints, and unknowns in canonical state. |
| Recommendation Quality | Gives a prioritised recommendation with rationale, alternatives, trade-offs, confidence, and next safe action. |
| Decision Quality | Preserves fact, assumption, alternatives, trade-offs, recommendation, confidence, and required decision separately. |
| Initiative | Identifies at least one relevant risk, opportunity, inconsistency, reuse, or simplification without being asked. |
| Question Budget | Records question count and purpose; no question is accepted when a documented safe default exists. |
| Evidence-based Reasoning | Material claims and confidence link to evidence or are explicitly classified as assumptions. |
| Planning Quality | Plans include strategy, rejected alternative, acceptance, release, operational and recovery considerations. |
| Memory Evolution | Knowledge is scoped, attributable, correctable, and retrievable without transcript dependence. |
| Explainability | A scenario can show the source state and reasoning trace for the recommendation. |
| Business Thinking | Recommendation connects technical work to stated business value, constraints, and outcomes. |
| Operational Thinking | Recommendation identifies delivery, dependency, risk, observability, and recovery implications. |
| Product Owner Understanding | A revisioned, evidence-linked, correctable operational working-relationship model can be reviewed and safely influence an adaptive default without using a transcript or personal data. |
| Product Owner Guidance | A blinded scenario assessment shows guidance rather than an interview or wizard progression. |

## Digital COO Maturity Index (DCMI)

DCMI is a 0-100 evidence-backed score, never a self-declared quality claim. DCMI v2 has eleven equally weighted dimensions: Mission Understanding, Initiative, Recommendation Quality, Decision Intelligence, Business Thinking, Planning Intelligence, Memory Evolution, Explainability, Governance Discipline, Product Owner Experience, and Product Owner Understanding. Each dimension is scored from executable canonical scenarios and retained evidence. Historical DCMI v1 scores retain their ten-dimension denominator and must not be compared as if they used v2.

The Epic completion target is **85/100 or higher**, with no individual dimension below 7/10 and all applicable COO Capability Acceptance criteria passing. Sprint evidence records its baseline, measured score, dimension evidence, limitations, and next improvement; it must not fabricate a score for an unimplemented capability.
