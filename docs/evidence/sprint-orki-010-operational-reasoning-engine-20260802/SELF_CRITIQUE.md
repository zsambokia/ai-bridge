# ORKI-010 Self Critique

**Result:** PASS with explicit maturity limits

## What is now proven

The system prevents the most important architectural shortcut: a Factory Chat
provider cannot make a direct recommendation a canonical record. Reasoning is
state-backed, evidence-referenced, versioned, project-scoped and explainable.

## What is not yet proven

- The gate proves contract completeness and deterministic behaviour, not the
  semantic merit of every provider-generated alternative.
- Cost, risk and long-term effect are structured textual assessments; they are
  not a calibrated forecasting or financial model.
- The current scenarios are intentionally bounded. ERP, enterprise,
  production-incident, multi-provider and cross-project-strategy scenarios
  remain necessary before claiming broad operational maturity.
- ORE does not yet convert reasoning into a governed plan or delivery action.

## Decision

These limits are safe because the engine fails closed, exposes its reasoning,
does not receive authority, and keeps DCMI at 66/100. They are not hidden by a
score increase or a claim of autonomous COO completion.
