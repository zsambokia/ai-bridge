# Integrated target architecture

The target separates durable Conversation and CSE authority from stateless
Context Assembly, Understanding and Evaluation. Immutable results flow to the
relevant Domain Authority; CSE may request Mission Resolution, which remains
separate from MSM Mission lifecycle ownership. The AI Kernel begins only after
Operational Foundation admission and is not Cognitive Processing.

FactoryIP is the L0–L4 foundation: L0 scope eligibility precedes semantic
retrieval; L1 Evidence and L2 Provenance are distinct; L3 Artifact,
Knowledge Candidate and published Knowledge are distinct; L4 Factory Packet
transport is distinct from delivery, interaction and payload. FFS resolves
names/bindings and does not carry payload traffic; zoning is the canonical
transport permission mechanism.
