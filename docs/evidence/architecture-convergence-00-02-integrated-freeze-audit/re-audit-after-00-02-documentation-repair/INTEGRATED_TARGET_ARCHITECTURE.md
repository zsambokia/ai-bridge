# Integrated target architecture

AI Kernel is execution/technical capability and is not Cognitive Processing. Conversation is durable and independent; CSE alone transitions it and routes it to Mission Resolution. Context Assembly, Understanding, and Evaluation are stateless and cannot mutate domain state; Evaluation has no authority. Mission Resolution is separate from knowledge publication. FactoryIP is the foundation: L0 firewall eligibility precedes semantic retrieval; L4 envelope is distinct from delivery/interactions and payload. Evidence and Provenance are distinct, as are Artifact, Claim, Evidence, and Knowledge candidate/object. FFS controls resolution. Canonical zoning is retained.
