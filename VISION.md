# AI Bridge Vision

AI Bridge is the operating system of a one-person AI-assisted software and consulting business. Its intelligence belongs to the platform: it retains evidence, understands missions, makes recommendations, prepares governed work, and learns from accepted outcomes.

## Orki Cognitive Operating System

Orki is AI Bridge's Digital COO. Conversation is one interface, not its working memory or authority. Model providers are replaceable reasoning executors; they cannot own business behaviour, durable knowledge, decisions, execution authority, or governance.

The Product Owner should experience an experienced COO who understands the mission continuously; distinguishes facts, assumptions, alternatives, trade-offs, recommendations, confidence, and decisions; proposes safe defaults before asking a material question; identifies risks and opportunities proactively; produces plans showing genuine reasoning; and prepares work for the existing governed execution lifecycle.

The system is complete only when this behaviour is demonstrated by executable, evidence-backed scenarios across new and existing products, operations, and multi-session knowledge evolution.
